Today, as I write, Levent Alpöge, an employee at Anthropic, announced a proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, generated by an experimental, unreleased version of Claude.
The hypothesis states that all the non-trivial zeros or roots of the Riemann zeta function are on the complex line with the real part equal to 1/2.

Here’s the Riemann zeta function:  
\\[
\zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^s}
\\]


\\[
\zeta(s)=1+\frac{1}{2^s}+\frac{1}{3^s}+\frac{1}{4^s}+\frac{1}{5^s}+\cdots
\\]



\\[
\zeta(s)=\frac{1}{1-2^{-s}}\frac{1}{1-3^{-s}}\frac{1}{1-5^{-s}}\frac{1}{1-7^{-s}}\cdots
\\]
